## External Stylesheet

Create an about page for your site or application and an external stylesheet to choose a palette of colors and fonts.   Write a few paragraphs explaining why you created your site or application, and talk about why you felt motivated to create it. Include an image of yourself (optional), and a logo if you'd like to have one. Create a color palette for your stylesheet by writing the colors in to the comments of your style sheet. Use colors from your palette for your page background color and text you use in your page. Include links to your social media presence or one for your business or application.
